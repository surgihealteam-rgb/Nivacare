What kind of home healthcare can NIVA CARE arrange?+

NIVA CARE can coordinate services such as home nursing, elder care, physiotherapy, doctor visits and post-hospital support, depending on availability and the patient’s requirements.

How do I know which service is right?+

Tell us what is happening, what support has been advised and what help the family needs. A care coordinator can guide you toward an appropriate option.

Can I speak to someone before booking?+

Yes. Call 6300695591 or use WhatsApp to explain your requirement before deciding on a service.

Is home healthcare suitable for emergencies?+

No. Home healthcare is for planned or ongoing care. For a medical emergency, contact your local emergency medical service or go to the nearest emergency department.
